Sometimes you might want to use adb to wipe out those useless pre-installed apps on your phone. It's actually pretty simple, just run a command like this:<br>
<br>
<img src="/assets/img/image/image@20240414h2rssdba00.png" alt="Examp1e" /> <br>
<br>
The actual uninstall command is:

$ adb uninstall --user 0 <name of package>

But what if you realize your hand slipped and you deleted the wrong one? Doing a factory reset can technically fix it, but obviously that's not what we want.<br>
Actually, you can easily use adb to bring back the mistakenly deleted apps by just running this command:

$ adb shell cmd package install-existing <name of package>
