## Intro

Recently found out that site.ac offers free domain registrations. The barrier to entry is super low, you don't need any personal information, just an email will do.

## Register an Account

### Open in your browser: https://site.ac

<img src="/assets/img/image/image@20240521gafdn00.png" alt="Examp1e" />

### Click the New Account button in the top right

<img src="/assets/img/image/image@20240521gafdn01.png" alt="Examp1e" />

### Fill in the registration info

<img src="/assets/img/image/image@20240521gafdn02.png" alt="Examp1e" />

### Click the New Domain Name button in the top left

<img src="/assets/img/image/image@20240521gafdn03.png" alt="Examp1e" />

### Enter the domain you want, and check the custom DNS-NS address box

<img src="/assets/img/image/image@20240521gafdn04.png" alt="Examp1e" />

### Set DNS-NS

Fill in the addresses provided by your DNS service provider (using DNSExit as an example here):
<img src="/assets/img/image/image@20240521gafdn05.png" alt="Examp1e" />

## Save updates, and the domain is ready to use.
