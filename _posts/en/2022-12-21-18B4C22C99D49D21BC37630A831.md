--Ты мой наркотик, я не могу остановиться.
<br>
The vast, uninhabited wilderness is terrifyingly silent.
<br>
A gust of cold wind howls past, kicking up dust that fills the sky.
<br>
Everything here is so expansive, so desolate. The broader it is, the more sorrowful it feels. Just like the depths of my heart, a wasteland, so lonely, so suffocating.
<br>
The withered grass has long lost all its leaves, leaving not a single trace behind. Only the dry stems remain, rustling like a mournful wail.
<br>
On the horizon, the sun sets in the west.
<br>
The lingering afterglow spills over the earth, dyeing it a faint fiery orange, yet it cannot mask the darkness, making this place look increasingly gloomy and terrifying.
<br>
I sit beside a tall tree trunk. The interwoven branches overhead block out the sunlight, obscuring the surrounding environment from my sight.
<br>
Behind me is an endless desert.
<br>
An endless desert, devoid of any growing vegetation, devoid of any living animals.
<br>
On the cracked tree trunks, the circling birds have long since failed to return. Only the interwoven dead branches remain, radiating a harsh, lifeless glare under the dark red, blood-like light of the setting sun. The extremely parched branches, having endured who knows how long of baptism, crumble into ash at the slightest touch, scattering tracelessly into the air.
<br>
Illuminated by the afterglow on the horizon, those dry tree trunks look like hideous, ugly faces, seemingly intent on swallowing me, sending shivers down my spine.
<br>
How could such a dry, bleak environment be associated with the mere word "desolate"?
<br>
No, this is not a desert, nor is it desolation. This is simply the road to the underworld!
<br>
Thick dust covers the gravel on the ground.
<br>
I reach out to pick up a stone, but it feels so heavy, so dense. Even if I exhaust all my strength, I cannot lift it; I only end up hurting my own hands.
<br>
I lower my head, watching my shadow cast into the crevices of the rocks. So thin, looking so frail, so miserable.
<br>
The ground has long since cracked dry. The fissures are covered in dust, much like my heart. And the obsession has never been let go.
<br>
Poignant beauty and bleakness have become the dominant tones here, both emotionally and environmentally.
<br>
I am alone between heaven and earth, imprisoned by the heavy sky and earth, destined never to raise my head.
<br>
I struggle to breathe; I am still alive.
<br>
Perhaps I want to live, but I do not even know why I want to live. Perhaps purely for the sake of living, because only in living is there hope. Only in living is there courage to face the unknown and despair, and the possibility of finding my own dawn and light.
<br>
Poignant beauty, sorrow, an indelible, un-washable melancholy permeates the space between heaven and earth. Mingled with hope, fused with endless decadence. I have been abandoned, abandoned in this boundless wilderness.
<br>
My gaze pierces through everything between heaven and earth, looking into the distance, yet I can see no end, nor can I see hope.
<br>
My soul is like those countless floating dead woods, drifting alone in the air, as if it could be annihilated at any moment.
<br>
But ultimately, I stand up. I am unwilling to just sit and wait for death.
<br>
I must find the hope to survive. Even if it is just a little, even if it is a slim hope, I must try once.
<br>
Slowly standing up, my legs are numb, almost causing me to fall, yet I stubbornly steady myself.
<br>
Walking forward step by step, stepping over fine gravel, making a crunching sound, like my utterly fragile heart.
<br>
The pace gradually quickens, turning into a run.
<br>
With no destination, my mind completely blank, without any thoughts, I run. Confused, running toward that endless direction. Run, run with all my might, before the sun sets, before that thread of light completely dissipates; run, run recklessly. If I fall, I will leave without any ties, departing alone; run, carrying hope, embracing despair, gripping the past in one hand, unwilling to let go, and grasping the present in the other, unwilling to let it slip away.
<br>
This world is so narrow, yet it has so many forks in the road. Once you stray, who knows where to go, or what path to take?
<br>
Running, emerging from the unknown, and running toward the vast unknown.
<br>
My vision becomes blurred, my consciousness chaotic. But I persist, running endlessly. My willpower is constantly sharpened, telling myself over and over: Run, do not stop, keep running. Only through constant escape and fleeing can one obtain freedom and hope.<br>
I just keep running like this. The wind tears my skin, cutting straight into my heart like a sharp blade.
<br>
I don't know how far I have run. I only remember feeling so tired, so exhausted. The scenery before my eyes changes, my vision blurs, and tears flow from the corners of my eyes, icy cold, bringing a tearing pain.
<br>
I know my tears will burn me, but regardless of the pain, only this way can I persist longer.
<br>
I thought there would be hope ahead, that there would be the light of life, but ahead is still pitch black, only a void where nothing can be seen, and there is no end.
<br>
I don't know how long I have run, I only feel my whole body weak and sore, intense pain radiating from my limbs and bones, almost unable to support my body.
<br>
And my reward is: an abandoned city, dilapidated buildings, rusted statues, fully displaying the glory of the past and the desolation of today.
<br>
My footsteps slow down. My lips tremble slightly, but I cannot speak. The empty city is incredibly vast, but there is no place for me. I don't know where to go.
<br>
My heart is trembling, my soul is trembling, and my body is trembling too.
<br>
Just wandering aimlessly, looking for a place to rest. Regrettably, the world is too vast. Even with endless crowds and magnificent cities, I am but a drop in the ocean, as insignificant as a grain of sand.
<br>
Between heaven and earth, a lifeless atmosphere fills the air. I walk like a walking corpse, incredibly dull, walking without a goal.
<br>
And then........
<br>
There probably is no "then". My heart has died along with it, leaving only a shell, an empty husk without any thought, a body that doesn't know how to move forward.
<br>
Down and out, I lean against a church, looking at that ray of blood-like setting sun.
<br>
The color of the setting sun is vivid, reflecting in my pupils, crimson like blood, yet resembling flames, burning.
<br>
The church is very old, very dilapidated. The walls are covered in moss and strange insect holes, making this originally solemn and majestic building look incredibly ruined.
<br>
I sit quietly on the steps outside the church, staring blankly at the setting sun, without weeping or wailing, just watching.
<br>
Do I want to cry? For this broken church?
<br>
I smiled, with no warmth in my eyes, but with endless sorrow, like an abandoned child.<br>
The setting sun is like blood, the bloody light spreads in my pupils.
<br>
I watch, my eyes aching severely. I clench my fists, my nails digging deep into flesh and blood, yet I still do not realize that my tears have long run dry.
<br>
The blood-red setting sun is like a bloody dying lamp. It is a key that opened the door to my heart, yet it refuses to give me a final window, leaving me in the wind and rain to be torn to pieces.
<br>
My palms are scratched with long, bloody marks, my fingertips stained crimson, yet I feel no pain.
<br>
Could this ruined city be my final resting place?
<br>
No, I don't want this, I am not resigned...
<br>
But so what? My voice is swallowed by the howling wind, sinking like a stone into the sea. "I stand at the edge of the cliff, but something always pulls me back, all the voices echoing in my head reminding me of everything I once had."
<br>
In the depths of my heart, there is a faint expectation and longing. My soul is drawn, step by step, stepping into this church.
<br>
I look at the giant oil painting on the church wall, a glimmer of hope flashing in my eyes.
<br>
I am still running, fantasizing if there is a dawn of hope behind this empty city.
<br>
My heart slowly turns cold. Disappointment, despair, fear... surge into my mind all at once.
<br>
My soul sways in the wind, seemingly about to disappear at any moment. I want to grab hold, but alas, I cannot. The wind howls, blowing up the broken hair on my forehead, blowing harder and harder, roaring past my ears, piercing my cheeks with pain.
<br>
My heart aches as if being torn apart; pain and despair intertwine, driving me to the brink of collapse.
<br>
I want to scream, I want to shout aloud, but I find my throat blocked by something, unable to make a single sound.
<br>
Even crying has become a luxury.
<br>
My soul is trapped in a cloud of fog, struggling, trying hard, but it is always in vain.
<br>
Even if I want to cry and make a scene, my soul is still trapped in that cloud of fog, and no matter what, I cannot break free.
<br>
I have grown used to the bitter struggles of these years, but when this moment truly arrives, I realize I am so helpless, so helpless that there is only despair.
<br>
My soul struggles uncontrollably. I want to leave, but I am trapped in the fog, unable to move an inch.
<br>
When the fog clears and the truth is revealed, hope finally falls through.
<br>
And what am I expecting?
<br>
The edge of the city leads to... another wilderness.
<br>
Reality is cruel.
<br>
Suffocating loneliness...
<br>
I stand outside the city, looking at this vast expanse of land before me. In an instant, I dazedly lose my direction.
<br>
Where should I go?
<br>
"Boom!" A loud clap of thunder, deafening.
<br>
A violent aura wildly spreads, rolling up yellow sand and gravel all over the ground, tumbling in the air. Dark clouds gather in the sky, lightning flashes and thunder rolls, as if a torrential downpour is imminent, yet a moment later it returns to calm once more.
<br>
I can feel that right now, I am like a trapped beast locked in a cage. My eyes stare straight ahead, without focus, and without vitality.
<br>
My world is pitch black, and I cannot see the path ahead at all. This world is filled with an eerie darkness, acting like a trap waiting for prey, waiting for them to jump in, waiting for them to die.
<br>
And I have become that prey.
<br>
My consciousness drifts continuously. Everything in front of me becomes distorted, like shadows swaying, like countless ghostly snakes flicking their scarlet tongues. I want to scream, but I cannot make a sound.
<br>
My body is dragged forward, but there is no one beside me.
<br>
I want to break free from that restraint, but I am pulled back by an even stronger suction force, falling to the ground once again.
<br>
I hear bursts of screams, like countless wronged souls roaring. I feel my body becoming lighter and lighter...
<br>
Finally, I can hold on no longer, falling to the ground, sinking into darkness...
<br>
A teardrop slides from the corner of my eye, dripping down my cheek to the ground, splashing a tiny ripple.
<br>
Immediately following is the heart-rending howl of the gale, screaming, wailing, as desolate as if from the netherworld, making one's blood run cold. My consciousness gradually awakens from the blur, yet I feel my body so oppressed I cannot breathe.
<br>
As if my hands and feet are bound by iron chains, I curl up on the freezing ground, kneeling, hands tied behind my back, as if suspended high in the air, my body convulsing violently.
<br>
Finally, I fall, falling into the endless wasteland, buried by despair. Up to this moment, I still haven't seen the dawn of hope. ...... It's getting dark, it's so cold...
<br>
I had imagined countless times, only to be let down countless times. How I hoped to meet a companion, even just one, just one, as long as there is one, a companion who could give me warmth when I am lonely, who I could rely on when I am helpless, someone to lean on each other in the boundless wilderness, letting the dark night be our backdrop... That would be enough. But... there was no one...
<br>
My heart grows numb in the cold.
<br>
I close my eyes, unwilling to open them again, just wanting to sleep like this.
<br>
Just keep sleeping like this, let it be...
<br>
Do not wake up again.
<br>
The profound night has fallen, a sky without twinkling stars, yet not the complete darkness I expected.
<br>
Only a solitary moon remains in the sky, coldly illuminating this world, illuminating my heart.
<br>
I look up, gazing at the crescent moon in the sky.
<br>
In that endless night, two bright stars are shining. My eyes fill with tears, sparkling along with them.
<br>
Why... is it because it's too lonely here, or is it because... I miss him too much.
<br>
I gaze up at the firmament overhead, seeing only dim stars.
<br>
Since all is void, I would rather close my eyes and sink into an illusion.
<br>
Watching the stars, feeling my own heartbeat.
<br>
"One, two, three..." Right before my eyes, those two stars are constantly drawing closer to each other; then, breaking through the barrier of millions of light years, they come together.
<br>
At that moment, the wind stopped, and billions of shooting stars roared down, forming a dazzlingly brilliant light in the sky, blooming in the dark night. In an instant, the starlight filled the sky, illuminating the entire world.
<br>
I watched this scene and smiled.
<br>
That was a beautiful picture. Such brilliant light, such dazzlingly radiant stars, I will never forget. <br>
"Would my star be among them?"
<br>
Using my last bit of strength, I struggled and walked toward the direction where the shooting stars fell.
<br>
Before my eyes, everything was pitch black.
<br>
But I felt no fear at all; instead, I felt an inexplicable excitement. At this moment, I seemed to feel I could touch a soft embrace. The corners of my mouth curled slightly, revealing a smile, and I reached out, wanting to touch it.
<br>
Below was a sparkling lake.
<br>
Another gust of wind blew, making one shiver.
<br>
Here, I could clearly smell the fishy, salty scent of aquatic weeds. The lake water was very cold, so cold that my heart also cooled down.
<br>
Leaning against that boulder that had not yet turned to dust, facing that meteor shower of flashing lights. <br>
I saw shooting stars streaking across, carrying brilliant light, blooming in mid-air. They danced in the sky, dazzling, like a dream, like an illusion...
<br>
I don't know how many stars perished that night, how many meteors fell in that grand banquet of the Milky Way. <br>
I only know that those stars were left for me by you...
<br>
My heart is trembling, not from fear, not from surprise, not from panic, but from happiness.
<br>
Since the person is already gone...
<br>
A last reluctant glance at this unprosperous world. Then, fading away with the shooting stars.
<br>
<br>
That day, in a desert, under the pitch-black night sky, a boy found liberation. He had a dream; he embraced his star and broke free from loneliness.
