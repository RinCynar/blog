I wish to be a speck of floating dust, carefree and unbound, drifting in the air, in the glimmering light, light and free. The soil firmly guards the earth, silently bearing the heavy burden of life, whereas I desire to float in mid-air, rising with the wind, accompanied by the wind, pursuing what I thirst for within it. Although the soil is full of fertility and life, it also binds the vigor of growth. I, however, do not wish to be bound, nor to be restricted by anything. There is nothing I seek in the soil; I only wish to soar in the vast sky, following the gentle breeze. There, there are no worries, no fetters, only the exhilaration of freedom.
<br>
The floating dust in the air may be small, but it is a symbol of freedom. So small that it can only be noticed with the help of other light, yet so insignificant. Drifting with the wind, unconstrained and unbound, between heaven and earth, shuttling through green mountains and verdant valleys, in the moment it is illuminated by light, it blossoms with the radiance of freedom, equality, and ideals. I, too, wish to become such floating dust, lightly passing through the shackles, and looking back to casually remark, "It is neither stormy nor sunny."
<br>
The drifting floating dust has a brief life, yet in that fleeting moment, it enjoys the joy of freedom to the fullest. It is lonely, therefore it is free; it is insignificant, therefore it is fearless; it has nothing, so anything it possesses will become everything to it. It does not need to worry about the future, nor fret about life. Even if doomsday approaches, it can still risk its all, using its nothingness in an attempt to win everything. Oh, floating dust, forever immersed in the joy of freedom, unburdened by fame and wealth, untroubled by merit and glory, merely chasing inner freedom and happiness.
<br>
Perhaps, being a speck of floating dust means loneliness, means not being noticed by others, but so what? We are already just a drop in the ocean in this vast world. Since the end of everything is nothingness, then on this journey, whether it is Trailblazing, Destruction, The Hunt, Preservation, Propagation, or Elation... all will sink into meaninglessness. Why not seek elation on this journey? To be lonely for freedom is always better than being bound in comfort. Because only in the free drifting, like floating dust, can one find one's true self, and discover the meaning of life and inner peace.
<br>
I wish to be free floating dust in the air. Small as it is, it is free and unfettered, chasing the light beneath the blue sky, catching a glimpse of its destiny amidst the vast misty waves, flying to its heart's content, rejoicing to the fullest, and thoroughly enjoying the joy of freedom. May the floating dust forever inspire the journey to be ever calm, and may the trickery never be exposed. We shall meet again in the dawn—the undying floating dust.
<br>
Giving up on questioning is like the heavy rain giving up on the piety of the stars, allowing the torrent of metaphors to pass through your body. You will see the truth at the end of the starry sky, an empty, lingering rose-colored mist.
