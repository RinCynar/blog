It is night.
<br>
The sky is pitch black. The sun has long since set, yet there is no moonlight.<br>
Not a single star remains in the dark sky, not even a sparse glimmer. The earth seems shrouded in a veil of gray ash, hazy and mysterious, like a canvas spread with white cloth. Though it has already been soaked in thick ink, everything remains so quiet and serene.<br>
But, when the time comes, this seemingly peaceful picture will be shattered, torn to pieces. We all understand this tacitly, merely waiting in silence.
<br>
A gust of cold wind blows past, carrying a trace of chill.
<br>
The black canopy of the night flows slowly like dark silk. The sky is extremely dim, devoid of any clouds, as if the entire city is enveloped in a gray smog. Through the window, only half of the sky can be seen. On the other half, the crescent moon resembles a scimitar, radiating a cold, eerie light, and perhaps a sky full of stars—yes, one can easily imagine that. Before seeing it with one's own eyes, an ideal starry sky, a sky that perhaps only exists in fairy tales, seemed so real in the heart for that brief second.<br>
If that paper window were truly pierced, it would only feel that the so-called dreams and reality are separated by thousands of mountains and rivers, vastly remote.
<br>
Or rather, all of this was but an illusion of a flower in the mirror, the moon in the water.
<br>
Let us not dwell on that for now. At this moment, the dim yellow light of the streetlamp indeed shines in from afar.<br>
There are no pedestrians on the street, and the streetlamp is merely lit for the sake of being lit. A gentle breeze brushes past, blowing down a scattering of withered branches and rotten leaves. The sound is somewhat eerie, making one's hair stand on end, like a cry from a strangled throat, so sorrowful, yet powerless to struggle.
<br>
The streetlamp's light is dim, and the shadows of the trees outside the window dance, swaying like phantoms in the night, as if meant to intimidate. Yet, inexplicably, it does not resemble a phantom, but rather a living creature prowling.<br>
In this tranquil and dark world, the light inside the window is faint, as if an invisible barrier exists between the night and the light, blocking all sources of light outside, leaving only wisps of gloomy, obscure darkness that breeds despair.<br>
Beneath the window lies a deep, narrow alley, dark and heavy... Oh no, that dark alley has long since left me, becoming a phantom that perhaps only exists in memory.
<br>
Everything exists, and everything no longer exists. Existing in fading, existing in birth, existing in existence, yet all of this should never have existed.
