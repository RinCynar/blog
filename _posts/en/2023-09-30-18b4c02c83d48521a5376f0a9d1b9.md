I lie in the embers of autumn leaves, swallowed by the whirlpool of life, unable to break free from the bleak shackles. The corners of my eyes are tinged with the crimson of fallen leaves swept up by the wind, as if the endless sorrow within is spilling out. Yet the bustling crowds around me feel like another world, so distant, out of reach.
<br>
I used to think that the departure and return of wild geese was the norm in life, but the reality before me tells me the cruel truth. Drifting seeds will eventually find a resting place, but I am trapped in a maze with no way out. I have closed the door, severed my connection with the outside world, leaving only loneliness and pain as my companions.
<br>
In those quiet moments, I listen to the whispers of the wind, as if voices from the past are gently murmuring in my ear. The fallen leaves dance in the breeze like fragments of time, evoking warm memories of the past deep within. Memories flood in like the tide, and the joyous songs and laughter of bygone days bloom in my mind, yet they also push me into an endless abyss of emotion.
<br>
The dark corners of my soul are filled with oppression and powerlessness. I try to struggle within, but it is like being trapped in a bottomless chasm. The desolation and stillness of autumn have solidified into the shape of my heart. Watching the wind blow the crimson fallen leaves feels like my cry for help against depression, yet it is in vain, because no one can understand the bitterness in my heart.
<br>
Pedestrians hurry by, while I lie in the embers, reduced to a powerless bystander. The sanctuary I once believed in is now merely a futile illusion, and I am just a lonely wanderer. Yearning for warmth and love that can never be touched; I am like a seed blown away by the wind, unable to find its roots.
<br>
The door I closed with my own hands has now become an insurmountable barrier. I am left all alone in the silence to face myself. I am lost in this withered world of fallen leaves. The wind howls in my ears, just like the private words of the past, stirring my attachment to the happy times of yesteryear. But the beauty has long departed, leaving only endless emptiness and helplessness.
<br>
Perhaps, loneliness is slowly making me numb, deeply drowning me in the ocean of memories, unable to extricate myself. The turmoils of the past are like an invisible mire, dragging me into the abyss, from which there is no escape.
<br>
Finally, amidst these embers, I find a trace of peace. I close my eyes and let the wind blow away my last bit of struggle. Perhaps, this is a form of escape, a way of facing emotions too heavy to bear. In this tranquil autumn, I merge as one with the pain in my body, still lying in the embers of fallen leaves, sinking into eternal darkness. In the end, only the echoes of sorrow remain.
