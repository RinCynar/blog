Gazing through the deep autumn waters across ten thousand miles,
<br>
The fallen flowers over a thousand mountains cannot be found.
<br>
Bathing in the warm sun and the spring breeze,
<br>
My heart longs for winter snow to cover the high peaks.
<br>
<br>
Not waiting for illness to bind me,
<br>
I seek out the Yellow Springs to find the traces myself.
<br>
My body is green like the wings of youth,
<br>
Yet my spirit lacks luster and shines no light.
<br>
<br>
Do not say that distraction ruins the mind,
<br>
The guiding echoes are already too late.
<br>
The departed holds no attachments,
<br>
Why wander here with a desire to take up the pen?
<br>
<br>
Walking to the very end of the mortal world,
<br>
I unexpectedly met a soulmate, an affection so profound.
<br>
I only hope to face death like a final game of chess,
<br>
Not wasting this ambition, fearing no spare effort.
